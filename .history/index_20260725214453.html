the garden begins

I'll build us a private garden, Mimi, where every flower knows Michelle Banton's name, where frangipani bloom just to remind you that beautiful things can still remain.

a place to be ourselves

I'll plant your favorite willow tree so we have a private place to be ourselves, to expose our true hearts to each other, while the little birds chirp and bunnies hop around.

space to breathe

I'll grow hydrangeas by your window for the days you must put yourself first, giving you the space you need right now so peace is the first thing you breathe when you leave.

not fully together, not yet

And though we can't fully be together right now, while you tend to your own emotions and the timing isn't as perfect as it will one day be, I'll stay.

a perfect match

Because I know that once everything is in place, we are a perfect match for each other.

every version of you

I want to learn every version of you: your boundaries, your healing, the beautiful storms beneath the soil.

what I'm waiting to give you

So if you ever ask me what I wait to give you, I'll tell you. A pearl-shaped engagement ring, as I spend my life making sure you always have somewhere safe to grow.
